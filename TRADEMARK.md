- **Forks and modified versions need their own name and logo.** If you distribute or host a modified version, or a fork, give it a distinct name and logo that can't be confused with Stick Engine.

## What you may do

- **Truthful references.** Say what your project is in plain words, for example "based on Stick Engine", "a fork of Stick Engine" or "made with Stick Engine", as long as it is accurate and your own name is the prominent one.

- **Commentary and news.** Reviews, articles, tutorials, videos, criticism, parody and academic work may name and show the marks to refer to the project.
- **Community use.** Fan sites and community groups may use the name to talk about the project, as long as they make clear they are unofficial.
